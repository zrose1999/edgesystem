Loading the desk

Every pick comes from a strategy. Each one is tracked in the open until it has proved it beats the closing line.

What a pick is

A pick is a side at a price, taken from the market at the moment the desk saw it. The price is locked and never edited. Grade A means the edge cleared the full bar for that sport and market; B cleared half of it.

Bet or tracking

Bet picks come from a strategy that has earned official status and carry a stake in units. Tracking picks are logged and graded the same way but stake nothing. Every strategy starts out tracking.

How strategies are judged

By closing line value: did the price we took beat the price at kickoff? Over hundreds of bets, beating the close is the most reliable sign of a real edge, and it shows up long before wins and losses settle down. A strategy goes official after 30 graded full-bar picks with an average edge over the close above zero and a t-stat of at least 1.5. It is retired after 30 if it is not beating the close. Win rate never promotes anything.

Market moved our way

On each open pick, the desk compares the price taken with the latest price. If the line has moved toward our side since, the ticket shows it in green. That is the closing line value building in real time.

Where the numbers come from

Prices from DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars and Pinnacle via The Odds API. Weather from weather.gov. Injury reports and depth charts from ESPN. Team ratings from nflverse and ESPN results. The desk updates about every hour on game days.
