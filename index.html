What a pick is

A pick is a side at a price, taken from the market at the moment the desk saw it. The price is locked and never edited. Grade A means the edge cleared the full bar for that sport and market; B cleared half of it.

Bet or tracking

Bet picks come from a strategy that has earned official status and carry a stake in units. Tracking picks are logged and graded the same way but stake nothing. Every strategy starts out tracking.

How strategies are judged

By closing line value: did the price we took beat the price at kickoff? Over hundreds of bets, beating the close is the most reliable sign of a real edge, and it shows up long before wins and losses settle down. A strategy goes official once its graded full-bar picks beat the close on average with a t-stat of at least 2.5 at 15 to 19 picks, or 2.0 from 20 picks on. Learned models that already passed the out-of-sample test need 15 picks at 2.0. A strategy is retired after 30 picks if it is not beating the close. Win rate never promotes anything.

Market moved our way

On each open pick, the desk compares the price taken with the latest price. If the line has moved toward our side since, the ticket shows it in green. That is the closing line value building in real time.

Trading on Kalshi

The desk prices every Kalshi game, spread and total contract against Pinnacle's fair price and against EDGE's own picks, after Kalshi's fee. A paper book logs every trade it would make. Real orders are placed only while your switch on the Trading tab is on and not paused, only from the sources you tick, sized from the starting balance you set. Hitting your stop loss or take profit ends the session; switching off and on starts a new one. Changes need your PIN.

Where the numbers come from

Prices from DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars and Pinnacle via The Odds API. Weather from weather.gov. Injury reports and depth charts from ESPN. Team ratings from nflverse and ESPN results. The desk updates about every hour on game days.
