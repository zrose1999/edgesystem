# Changelog

- 2026-10-15: College football spots (ncaaf_spots.py, pattern ncaaf-spot) from a 9-season CFBD study run on GitHub (ncaaf_research.py). discover.py: discovery engine over ESPN histories, EDGE's archive and Kalshi marks; candidates reported in stats/discovery.md for the weekly review. - 2026-10-15: Soccer build-out. 12-season study (20,560 matches): early prices overreact to last-match luck (t -9.4) and to points above expected points (t -10); high last-match totals push overs (t 6); even matches lift the draw (t 15). soccer_form.py keeps current Understat form for the 5 leagues; edge_stack.py's soccer drift now includes these effects on top of the league drifts (stats/backtest_soccer_spots.md). tennis.py added (ESPN history, surface Elo, fatigue).
- 2026-10-15: Build-out round 1. move_nhl.py: NHL moneyline and total line-move patterns (the strongest move signals found: t 7 and 13, stable across halves). edge_stack.py: edge-stack (measured patterns + opening drift + NHL home back-to-back, summed) and sharp-drift (Pinnacle plus expected drift vs retail). Opening drift measured for NHL, NCAAF, NBA and five soccer leagues (stats/backtest_drift.md). NFL schedule spots tested on the 184 games ESPN still has opens for: nothing measurable. NFL stats-model flagged: against the close kRaw -0.16 (se 0.11) and its side went 42-57 on 6+ point gaps; proposed to Zack.
- 2026-10-14: Every sport's loop is now tracked. archive.py moves finished games (3 days after start; 30 for games with a pick or lean) out of ledger.json into permanent per-sport monthly files, ledger/archive/<YYYY-MM>/<sport>.jsonl, with every book's open, last pre-start and close price and the score: EDGE's own history for every sport. loop_check.py writes stats/loop_status.json each run (prices, Pinnacle coverage, results, closes, picks by strategy, graded, history per sport) and notify.py pushes an alert when a sport's loop breaks. Soccer research history (football-data and Understat, 5 leagues, 2022-2026) committed to stats/history/soccer.
- 2026-10-14: Fast runs: every 15 minutes, prices only for sports with a game in the next 3 hours, then the price-gap scan, injury and goalie news, Kalshi and alerts. They stop when credits fall under 6,000 (EDGE_FAST_RESERVE). Soccer lineup study (Understat XIs, 5,764 matches, 5 leagues, 2022-2026): a missing regular moves Pinnacle 0.9 points of win probability per unit of xG+xA per 90 above replacement (se 0.08, every season 0.8 to 1.1); even big absences move under 1 point, below any bar and Kalshi's fee, so no lineup pattern for now.
- 2026-10-14: Audit fixes. Audit finding: at hourly snapshots, US books and Kalshi track Pinnacle closely: no same-line gap of 1.5%+ outside two NBA games, so price gaps alone produce almost no picks in soccer, tennis, NHL, NFL or NCAAF.
