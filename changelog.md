First run: 57 survivors (56 move, 1 result). Without being told, it re-found three patterns EDGE already trades (NBA away back-to-back, NCAAF home favourite 21+, NHL total moves at 5.5/6.5) and added new ones (e.g. NHL away team with more rest, NCAAF home team off a long rest, NBA rest edge either way, soccer total and draw drifts by month/price). Stake 0; kalshi.py treats mirror:scan-* as track-only. Tested locally: 22 reads stamped (all leans: remaining moves under the minimums), rerun stamps 0; picks.json restored. memory/summary.md gains a scanner line (strategies tracking, graded scan reads, their CLV). Workflow (main): scan.py after discover.py in the morning run; scan.py stamp after ncaaf_spots.py. stats/scan/ is job output; the first strategies land with tomorrow's 11:07Z run.
