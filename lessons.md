# Lessons

Written in the Tuesday review. Read at the start of every read. Newest first. Each lesson: date, what happened, what to do differently.

- 2026-11-03: First full week under the Pinnacle gate. Counted graded reads 1,088 at -0.93% (Pinnacle close -0.88%, n 1,016); this week's 221 at -0.29% (t -1.05), non-parked 206 at -0.07% (last week's reads -1.11%). Gate-passed picks +0.99% (n 93, t 4.05); all passed +0.8% (n 112) vs gated -0.4% (n 84) vs unchecked -1.5% (n 32): the gate separates. sharp-soft is the first pattern near the bar (13 graded full-bar picks, +2.8%, t 2.45; needs 2 more at t >= 2.5). Loss review: 177 reads lost to the close this week, -484 pts, half price paid (-243) and half market move (-242); today's rules block 83 (71 gated, 12 parked). What the rules did not catch: 31 of the 32 "unchecked" reads are NHL totals where Pinnacle hangs 6.0 and the US book 5.5 or 6.5, so the gate cannot compare and lets them count; they lose -1.45% (t -4.4, both halves), all of it vig (price -1.95%, move +0.5%). What to do differently: a read the gate cannot check has not passed it; compare on the same number or do not count it (proposal in the 11-03 review PR). Findings: totals keep losing on fresh reads (-0.7% vs +1.1%, n 26, t -3.1); NCAAF dogs and 2+ days rest did not confirm on fresh reads. Ideas: inside the price-beating patterns, reads priced at the slow books added 10-28 (BetRivers, Hard Rock, theScore) +1.5% (n 60, t 4.9, both halves) vs DK/FD/MGM/Caesars -0.0% (n 31): the same price edge, but the market keeps moving against FanDuel's off-prices (-2.1% move), so not every "better than Pinnacle" price is stale. Samples are still small.

- 2026-10-20: First week with graded reads: 213 graded (83 picks, 130 leans), mean CLV -1.95% (t -9.1), 104-109; picks alone 37-46, -1.95%. No pattern is near promotion: the most graded full-bar picks are stats-model 7 and news-lag 5, both negative; learned-nhl-ml is the one early positive (+0.8% on 12 reads, t 1.2) but has 0 graded full-bar picks. CLV is net of the price paid, so a read with no information at -110 scores about -2.4%; on our totals the line barely moved (mean open-to-close under 0.5 pt in NCAAF, NFL and NHL), so most of the gap is the vig, not the direction. What to do differently: before trusting a learned model's live check, confirm it compares the same line at open and close. nhl_over's live check showed t 2.1, but all of it came from 5 games where the total moved a full goal (same-line games: corr 0.05, top-call move +0.2%, t 0.3, n 25). Likewise check that every Kalshi trade's ticker names the same teams as its event before reading its CLV (3 FCS tickers were filed under FBS games this week). Samples are small; no rule change.

- 2026-10-06: Before stamping, state which team the news hurts and check that the fair line moved toward the other team. A team losing its starter gets worse, so the fair line moves toward the opponent. Size each news event separately. The Tampa example that made Dallas shorter after a Bucs quarterback loss was backwards.
- 2026-10-06: Week 5 blends (points scored and allowed) produced 15 "edges" that were all public information. stats.py backtest confirms team stats alone do not beat the close (k = 0, stats side 38-55 at 6+ point gaps). Do not pick from stats gaps. Look for news, timing, and weather.
- 2026-10-06: Tampa Bay at Dallas moved from -10 to -8.5 on the Mayfield report before any read. By the time news is public on Monday, the first move is done. The edge is in the next piece of news (Wednesday status, Friday final, Sunday inactives) before the line moves again.
